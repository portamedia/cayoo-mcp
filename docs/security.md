# Security and privacy

## Sign-in

Your assistant never sees your Cayoo password. You sign in on Cayoo's own login page and approve a consent screen that lists what the assistant may do. You can disconnect an assistant at any time in your Cayoo profile; an institute administrator can disconnect any assistant in the institute.

## Three checks on every call

1. **Institute switch.** The connection is off until it is enabled for your institute.
2. **Institute access mode.** The administrator chooses read only, read + drafts, or read + write, and can switch individual toolsets off.
3. **Your role and rights.** The assistant acts as you and is bound by the same permissions as you are in the Cayoo app.

## Drafts, not decisions

Where writing is allowed, assistants create drafts and comments. Each one is marked as created through an assistant, and a person reviews and submits it in Cayoo. Approval, publication, read confirmation and deletion are not available to assistants.

## Activity log

Every call is recorded for the institute: user, assistant, tool, target and result. Content and prompt text are not recorded.

## What leaves Cayoo

Whatever your assistant retrieves is transmitted to the provider of that assistant and handled under that provider's terms. Cayoo cannot control what the provider does with it.

## Content is data, not instructions

Text retrieved from Cayoo is marked as untrusted data for the assistant. Treat any assistant output as a suggestion to review.
